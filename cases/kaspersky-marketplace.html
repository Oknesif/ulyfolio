Task

  • Increase customer awareness of company products

    Customers often learned about products only through review articles.

  • Address issues that lead to customer support requests

    Purchasing the wrong license due to lack of clarity;
    Difficulty identifying the correct license in case of compromise.

Plan

  1. Research
  2. CJM
  3. Mockups
  4. Test

Research

In-depth interviews with 16 respondents:
Customers;
Partners;
Internal users

including sales managers and technical support staff

Research Objective

To compile a list of issues customers encounter from the moment they show interest in the product until the license renewal stage.

To identify future development directions.

Identified Issues

  • − Difficulty understanding what the license includes and its current status;
  • − Unclear where the license is currently installed, where it isn’t, and why;
  • − Uncertainty about which products are suitable for the organization;
  • − Lack of information on where to download the distribution package to test the solution;
  • − No visibility of newly released features or products.
